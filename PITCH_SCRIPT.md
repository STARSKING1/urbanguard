# UrbanGuard Resilience — 2-Minute Shipathon Pitch Script 🎙️

## [0:00 - 0:30] The Problem
"When natural disasters hit or central power grids collapse, traditional cellular networks fail instantly. Citizens are left isolated without emergency updates or communication tools, and loved ones have no way to verify safety."

## [0:30 - 1:15] The Solution & Live Demo
"Meet **UrbanGuard Resilience**. 
1. **Grid-Active Mode:** Real-time hazard mapping powered by Supabase PostGIS and OpenStreetMap tracks active risk zones.
2. **Grid-Down Mesh Mode:** When cell towers go down, UrbanGuard turns nearby devices into an offline peer-to-peer relay network using Bluetooth Low Energy and Wi-Fi Direct—passing emergency SOS signals from node to node without internet access."

## [1:15 - 1:45] RevenueCat Monetization Strategy
"Monetization is driven by **RevenueCat SDK**:
- **Free Tier:** Access local hazard feeds, official emergency broadcasts, and manual safety status pings.
- **Resilience Pro ($4.99/mo):** Unlocks multi-hop BLE mesh relaying, family circle geofencing, and offline AI triage assistance. RevenueCat native paywalls seamlessly manage entitlements."

## [1:45 - 2:00] Conclusion
"UrbanGuard bridges the critical gap between grid connectivity and grid breakdown. Built with Flutter, Supabase, and RevenueCat for Shipathon 2026."
